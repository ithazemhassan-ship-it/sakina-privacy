Privacy Policy — Sakīna

Last updated: August 2026

Sakīna ("the app") is a dhikr (Islamic remembrance) reminder app. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data collection

Sakīna does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, and no advertising SDKs.

Location

The app uses your device's location, with your permission, only to calculate the Qibla direction and local prayer times. This location data is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted anywhere, stored remotely, or shared with any third party.

Hijri date lookup

To show an accurate Islamic calendar date, the app may send today's Gregorian date (no other information) to a public Hijri-date conversion API. If this fails or is unavailable, the app falls back to an offline calculation. No personal or device-identifying data is included in this request.

Notifications and exact alarms

The app schedules local dhikr and prayer-time reminder notifications at times you configure, using Android's exact-alarm capability so reminders arrive precisely on schedule. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on-device.

Data storage

Your preferences (reminder schedule, sound choice, language, and similar settings) are stored locally on your device only, using standard Android app storage. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Third parties

Sakīna does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking service.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL.
